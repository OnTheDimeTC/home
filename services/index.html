RACE DIRECTOR RESOURCES

Frequently Asked Questions

Planning a race? Here are answers to common questions about working with OTD.

What information do I need to request a quote?

Share your event date, location, race distance or distances, estimated participant count, and the services you need. We can discuss the right package for your event.

Can OTD work with RunSignup and MileSplit?

OTD offers RunSignup and MileSplit integration. Tell us which platform your event uses so we can confirm the setup for your race.

Where can participants find race results?

Use the Live Results page for current and upcoming meets and Historical Results for past events.

Do you support smaller community races?

Yes. Our published pricing includes packages starting at up to 50 registrants, with larger packages for events with more participants.

Can I request extras such as custom bibs or medals?

Yes. Optional event merchandise, custom bibs, engraved medals, and a premium finish setup are listed under Additional Features on this page.
