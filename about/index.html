OUR TECHNOLOGY

DAISY-TECH V3

Daisy-Tech combines electronic elapsed-time recording, barcode bib scanning and custom software. Finish times are recorded as runners cross the line and matched to their bib numbers. The system then calculates placements and publishes results in real time.

Unlike RFID chip timing, Daisy-Tech uses crew-assisted finish identification rather than automatic chip detection. This reduces equipment costs while maintaining electronic processing and results publishing. Automatic finish recording is a future development goal, not a current feature.
